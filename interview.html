Why this exists

The written paper is done. The interview is now the whole game.

If you've already been called for the offline IIITH interview, the next few conversations matter more than any mock test ever did. This cohort is built for that exact moment.

We are opening a free, selective program for 10 students who want practice that actually feels like the room they are about to walk into.

Who should apply
  • Students who have been shortlisted for the UGEE offline interview
  • Students who want direct mock-pressure before the final round
  • Students willing to share their SUPR and REAP scores for selection
How we select
  • We review your application, scores, and scorecard
  • We prioritize fit, urgency, and where we can add the most value
  • Selected students receive the next instructions on email
Before You Apply
The questions that matter right now

Is this free?

Yes. There is no payment. This is a free, limited cohort for 10 selected students.

What do I need to submit?

Basic contact details, your SUPR score, your REAP score, and an image of your scorecard.

Does applying guarantee a seat?

No. The form is an application, not an instant confirmation. We review and select the final 10.

What happens after I apply?

We send a confirmation email immediately. If selected, you receive the next steps on the same email.
