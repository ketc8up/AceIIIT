Exam Strategy

Knowing When to Move On Is a Skill

Competitive exams aren't only about solving questions. They're also about choosing which questions deserve your time.

Three Question Types

  • Immediate: You know the approach.
  • Think: You need some work.
  • Leave: The time cost is too high.

Decision Framework

Do I understand the question? → Can I see a viable approach? → Estimate the effort. Low → Solve, High → Mark / Move.

What to Measure

Your mock data can reveal: Questions abandoned, Questions taking too long, Incorrect answers after long attempts, Easy questions skipped, Revisits.
